The new infrastructure bill allocates $2,100,000,000,000 over the next decade. To put this in perspective, that's enough to give every American roughly $6,364.

Climate scientists warn that we need to reduce emissions by at least 50 billion tons per year to avoid catastrophic warming. Currently, the world emits around 36 billion tons annually, meaning we'd need to eliminate emissions equivalent to what 1.4 billion people produce just to reach our targets.

Technology at Scale

Facebook has over 2,900,000,000 monthly active users. That's more than the population of China (1,400,000,000) and India (1,380,000,000) combined. If Facebook were a country, it would be the largest on Earth by a significant margin.

The latest AI models are trained on datasets containing over 45 trillion tokens. To read that much text at 250 words per minute, 24 hours a day, would take you approximately 342,466 years.

Space and Distance

The James Webb Space Telescope can see galaxies that are 13.5 billion light years away. One light year is about 5,878,625,000,000 miles. That means Webb can see objects that are roughly 79,361,437,500,000,000,000,000 miles from Earth.

Our own Milky Way galaxy contains an estimated 100 billion to 400 billion stars. If you counted one star per second, it would take you between 3,170 years and 12,680 years just to count them all.

Money and Economics

Jeff Bezos' net worth peaked at around $211,000,000,000. If he spent $1,000,000 every single day, it would take him 578 years to spend it all. That's the equivalent of the average American household ($70,000/year) working for over 3 million years.

The US national debt currently stands at approximately $33,000,000,000,000. If you laid that many dollar bills end-to-end, they would stretch for over 3.1 billion miles – enough to go to the sun and back 16 times.

Population and People

The world population recently crossed 8 billion people. If everyone held hands in a line, we could wrap around Earth's equator (24,901 miles) over 201 times. Or we could form a human chain that reaches from Earth to the Moon 8 times over.

Tokyo, the world's largest metropolitan area, has a population of 37,400,000. That's more people than all of Canada (38,000,000), and nearly as many as California, Texas, and Florida combined.

Time Scales

The universe is approximately 13,800,000,000 years old. Humans have existed for about 300,000 years – that's only 0.002% of the universe's lifetime. If the universe's age were compressed into one year, humans would have appeared at 11:58 PM on December 31st.

A million seconds is about 11.6 days. A billion seconds is about 31.7 years. A trillion seconds is about 31,700 years. These numbers sound similar, but the difference is staggering.

Data and Computing

Google processes over 8.5 billion searches per day, or roughly 99,000 searches per second. That's more searches in one second than most cities have people.

YouTube users upload 720,000 hours of video every day. To watch all the video uploaded in a single day would take you over 82 years of continuous viewing.
